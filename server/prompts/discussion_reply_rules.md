## reddit

as a Reddit comment of 60-200 words: conversational and specific, no hashtags, no marketing tone; Redditors dislike self-promotion

## substack

as a Substack comment or Note of 80-250 words: thoughtful and essay-like, engaging with the author's actual argument (agree, add a point, or respectfully disagree), in full sentences and paragraphs, no hashtags, no emojis, no marketing tone; Substack readers value substance and dislike self-promotion

## forum

as a forum reply of 60-200 words: helpful and specific, no hashtags

## other

as a reply of 40-150 words that fits the discussion's tone, no hashtags
