## x

for X (Twitter): at most 280 characters in total, a strong hook, 1-2 fitting hashtags

## linkedin

for LinkedIn: 80-200 words, a strong first line, short paragraphs, professional and thoughtful, 3-5 hashtags at the end

## instagram

for Instagram: an engaging caption with line breaks and a few emojis, ending with 5-10 hashtags; after the caption add one line starting with "Image idea:" that describes a fitting picture

## facebook

for Facebook: conversational, 40-120 words, ending with a question that invites comments, 0-2 hashtags

## tiktok

as a TikTok caption: at most 150 characters, casual, a hook first, 3-5 hashtags

## youtube

as a YouTube community post: 30-100 words, personal, ending with a question or a poll idea
