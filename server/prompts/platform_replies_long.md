## x

on X (Twitter), at most 280 characters in total; use most of them

## linkedin

as a detailed LinkedIn comment of 150-200 words in 2-3 short paragraphs, professional and constructive; answer several of the post's points, not only one. Reach this length even if the style asks for short texts

## instagram

as an Instagram comment of 4-7 sentences, warm; an emoji is fine. Reach this length even if the style asks for short texts

## facebook

as a detailed Facebook comment of 150-300 words in 2-4 short paragraphs, conversational; answer several of the post's points, not only one. Reach this length even if the style asks for short texts

## tiktok

as a TikTok comment of at most 150 characters, casual; use most of them

## youtube

as a detailed YouTube comment of 150-300 words in 2-4 short paragraphs, friendly and on topic; answer several of the post's points, not only one. Reach this length even if the style asks for short texts
