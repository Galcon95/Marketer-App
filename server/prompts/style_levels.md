## 0

Style: polished, persuasive marketing copy is fine.

## 25

Style: clear and engaging; avoid the most worn-out marketing clichés.

## 50

Style: write like a thoughtful human copywriter: vary sentence length, use contractions and concrete details, open with a concrete statement rather than a question, say what something is directly instead of contrasting it with what it is not, choose one precise adjective over a string of them, and use at most one exclamation mark.

## 75

Style: sound like a real person writing it themselves, e.g. a reader who loved the book: plain everyday words, first person where it fits, a genuine opinion, short and specific, slightly informal is fine. No marketing clichés, no hype words, no exclamation-mark chains, emojis and hashtags only as many as the platform strictly needs, no em dashes.
